Privacy Policy

Your health data is sensitive. Here's exactly how we handle it.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

PulsingLabs ("we", "us") is an app for booking home blood tests in Pakistan. A licensed partner laboratory collects your sample at home and does the testing; we take the booking and payment, and show you your results with plain-language explanations. PulsingLabs is based in Karachi, Pakistan. Questions about this policy: contact@pulsinglabs.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account: your name and email from Google sign-in, phone number, date of birth and biological sex. We use age and sex to judge your results against the right ranges. We never see your Google password.
  • Health information: your test results, pregnancy status (if you tell us), height and weight (if you add them), and your answers to the optional wellbeing questionnaire (mood, stress, sleep, lifestyle).
  • Bookings: the package, date and time, your home address and map pin for the visit, and booking history.
  • Payments: the amount, status and payment reference. Card and wallet details are entered on Safepay's secure page; we never see or store your card number.
  • Family members: the name, date of birth, sex and results of relatives you add and book for.
  • AI chat: your recent conversations with the health assistant (up to your last 5, each with a short running summary).
  • Device and settings: a notification token so we can send you alerts, your consent choices, and lab ratings you give.

3. How we use it

  • To arrange your home visit and send your booking to the partner lab.
  • To show your results, explain them for your age and sex, and track how they change over time.
  • To answer your questions in the AI health assistant.
  • To send booking, payment and results messages by email and notification. A hepatitis result is never shown in a notification or an automatic email; you see it in the app.
  • To process payments, refunds and invoices.
  • Only if you tick "Send me offers from PulsingLabs" when you sign in: to send occasional offers, some on behalf of health partners, chosen using your age, sex and your own latest results. It is off unless you tick it, and you can turn it off any time in your profile. Hepatitis results are never used to choose who gets an offer.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud.

4. Why we are allowed to use it

We use your information to provide the service you booked, with your consent for optional features (offers, the wellbeing questionnaire, notifications), and where needed to keep the service safe and meet our legal and tax obligations. You can withdraw consent for optional features at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share only what each service needs:

  • Partner laboratory: your name, age, sex, phone number, home address and the tests booked, so they can collect and test your sample and send back the results.
  • Safepay: to take your payment.
  • Google: sign-in, and the Gemini AI that powers the health assistant. When you use the assistant, your first name, age, sex, results and booking details are sent with your question. Your email, phone number and address are not.
  • Hosting and messaging: Supabase (database and sign-in), Render (our server), Firebase (notifications and app crash reports), Sentry (server error reports), Brevo (email) and Cloudflare (this website). Crash and error reports contain technical details only; names, emails, phone numbers and results are removed before they are sent.
  • Health partners (only if you opted in to offers): we choose who receives an offer ourselves. The partner never receives your name, contact details or results, and never learns who received the message.

6. Your privacy at work

If you join your employer's PulsingLabs programme, HR can see whether you have booked and completed your health check. HR never sees your test results. Group health trends are shown to HR only once at least 5 colleagues have been tested, without names, and hepatitis results are never included. Nothing about family members you book for is ever shared with your employer.

7. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers that may be outside Pakistan, including in India, Singapore, Germany and the United States. By using PulsingLabs you agree to this. We protect it the same way wherever it is stored.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. You can ask to delete your account in your profile. Once we approve the request, usually within 30 days, we erase your health data, questionnaire answers, chat history, family members and notification tokens. We keep payment records and invoices, without your health data, for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

9. Your rights

  • See and download your data: Profile, then "Download my data".
  • Correct it: edit your profile, or contact us.
  • Delete your account and data: Profile, then "Delete my account & data".
  • Withdraw consent: turn off offers or notifications in the app.

To use any of these rights, or to complain, email contact@pulsinglabs.com.

10. Children

You must be 18 or older to create an account. Parents and guardians can add children as family members and book for them, and are responsible for the information they add.

11. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to your records is limited to you and to the staff who need it to run your booking, and every staff view of a patient record is logged.

12. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics on it. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and Fontshare, which can see your IP address, and our host, Cloudflare, may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the site from attacks. The PulsingLabs app does not use cookies; it keeps your sign-in and settings on your phone.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes we will tell you in the app or by email.

14. Contact

PulsingLabs, Karachi, Pakistan. Email: contact@pulsinglabs.com.
