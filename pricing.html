Vitals measured at your visit
PulseResting heart rate

Checks how fast your heart beats while you are resting. A steady pulse shows your heart is working comfortably, while an unusually fast or slow one is worth a closer look. Our phlebotomist counts it at your visit.

Blood pressure, top numberSystolic

Checks the pressure in your blood vessels each time your heart beats. This is the top number. High blood pressure usually causes no symptoms but quietly strains your heart, kidneys and eyes, so knowing your number helps you act early. Measured at your visit.

In Standard and Premium

Blood pressure, bottom numberDiastolic

Checks the pressure in your blood vessels between heartbeats, when your heart is resting. This is the bottom number. Read together with the top number, it shows whether your blood pressure is in a healthy range. Measured at your visit.

Blood count
HaemoglobinAnaemia check

Checks the protein in your red blood cells that carries oxygen around your body. Low levels (anaemia) can leave you tired, weak or breathless, and it is very common, especially in women. This test shows whether your blood is carrying enough oxygen.

In Standard and Premium

RBC countRed blood cells

Counts your red blood cells, the cells that deliver oxygen to every part of your body. Together with haemoglobin it helps explain why you might feel tired or look pale, and whether anaemia could be the reason.

In Standard and Premium

WBC countInfection and immunity

Counts your white blood cells, your body's defence against infection. The number often rises when you are fighting an infection and can drop after some illnesses, so it gives a quick picture of how your immune system is doing.

In Standard and Premium

Platelet countClotting cells

Counts the tiny cells that help your blood clot when you cut yourself. Too few can make you bruise or bleed easily, and too many can go with inflammation or low iron. It shows whether your blood clots the way it should.

In Standard and Premium
Blood sugar
Random blood sugarSugar at any time of day

Checks the sugar (glucose) in your blood at the time of your visit, with no fasting needed. It is a quick way to spot high blood sugar, an early sign of diabetes. If it is high, a follow-up test such as HbA1c confirms what is going on.

In Standard and Premium

HbA1cYour 3-month sugar average

Checks your average blood sugar over the last 2 to 3 months, not just today. It is the main test for spotting diabetes and pre-diabetes, and for seeing how well diabetes is controlled. Diabetes is very common in Pakistan and often has no early symptoms.

Cholesterol the full profile
Total cholesterolAll the cholesterol in your blood

Checks the total amount of cholesterol, a fatty substance, in your blood. Your body needs a little, but too much can slowly build up in your arteries and raise the risk of a heart attack or stroke. This is the headline number for your heart health.

In Standard and Premium

LDL cholesterolThe "bad" cholesterol

Checks the 'bad' cholesterol, the kind that can stick to the walls of your arteries and narrow them over time. It is the cholesterol number doctors watch most, and keeping it low helps protect your heart and brain.

In Standard and Premium

HDL cholesterolThe "good" cholesterol

Checks the 'good' cholesterol, which carries extra cholesterol away from your arteries and back to your liver. Higher is better here, and regular exercise and not smoking help raise it.

In Standard and Premium

LDL/HDL ratioHeart-risk balance

Compares your 'bad' cholesterol with your 'good' cholesterol. It gives a clearer picture of your heart risk than either number alone: the lower the ratio, the better.

In Standard and Premium

TriglyceridesFat in your blood

Checks the most common type of fat in your blood. Extra sugar, sweets, white flour and fried food are turned into triglycerides, and high levels add to the risk of heart disease. It shows how your everyday diet is affecting your blood.

In Standard and Premium
Thyroid
TSHThyroid screen

Checks how hard your brain is asking your thyroid, a small gland in your neck, to work. Your thyroid controls your energy, weight and mood. A high result can point to a slow thyroid (tiredness, weight gain, feeling cold) and a low one to an overactive thyroid (weight loss, a racing heart). Thyroid problems are common, especially in women.

In Standard and Premium

Free T3Active thyroid hormone

Checks the most active thyroid hormone, the one that acts directly on your cells. Together with TSH and free T4 it shows whether your thyroid is making the right amount, which can explain a racing heart, weight loss or restlessness. It can dip when you are unwell, so it is read with your other results.

Free T4Thyroid hormone level

Checks the main thyroid hormone that is free in your blood and available to your body. Together with TSH it shows whether your thyroid is making too much or too little, which can explain feeling very tired, cold or anxious, or losing or gaining weight.

Liver and infection
SGPT (ALT)Liver health

Checks a liver enzyme that leaks into your blood when liver cells are under strain. A raised level can come from fatty liver, hepatitis, alcohol or some medicines. It is an early signal to look after your liver, not a diagnosis on its own.

In Standard and Premium

Total bilirubinLiver and bile ducts

Checks a yellow substance your liver clears from your blood after old red blood cells break down. A high level can turn the skin and eyes yellow (jaundice) and may point to a liver or bile-duct problem. Mildly raised levels are also seen in some healthy people, so it is read with your liver enzyme.

Premium only

Anti-HCVHepatitis C screening

Checks for hepatitis C, a virus that infects the liver and spreads through blood, for example through unsafe injections. Many people carry it for years without symptoms, and it can now be cured. A reactive result is confirmed with a second test.

Premium only

HBsAgHepatitis B screening

Checks for hepatitis B, a virus that infects the liver. It spreads through blood and from mother to baby, and many people do not know they have it. A vaccine protects against it and treatment can keep it under control. A reactive result needs follow-up with a doctor.

Kidney
CreatinineKidney health

Checks a waste product your muscles make every day, which healthy kidneys filter out of your blood. When the kidneys are not filtering well it builds up, so this is one of the main ways to check how your kidneys are doing.

In Standard and Premium

UreaKidney waste product

Checks a waste product made when your body breaks down protein, which healthy kidneys filter out of your blood. A high level can mean your kidneys are working harder than they should, although dehydration or a high-protein diet can also raise it, so it is read with creatinine.

Bones and vitamins
CalciumBones, nerves and muscles

Checks the mineral that keeps your bones and teeth strong and helps your muscles, nerves and heart work properly. Low calcium often goes with low vitamin D, and too much or too little can cause problems, so this checks you are in the healthy middle.

In Standard and Premium

Vitamin DBone and muscle strength

Checks the vitamin that helps your body absorb calcium and keeps your bones and muscles strong. Low vitamin D is very common in Pakistan, even with plenty of sunshine, and can cause tiredness and aches. It is easy to put right once you know.

Iron and inflammation

Checks how much iron your body has stored. It is the best test for low iron, which is very common, especially in women and children, and can make you tired and breathless even before your haemoglobin drops.

Pricing questions

Is the app free?

Yes. Downloading and using PulsingLabs is free. You pay only for the check-up you book, and the price includes the home visit. There is no subscription.

Do I need to fast before the test?

No. Every test in both packages is non-fasting, so you can eat and drink as usual before your visit.

Who are the packages for?

Everyone, at any age. Standard is a clear first check-up; Premium adds 10 more tests for a fuller picture. You can also book for family members from your account.

Which cities do you cover?

Home sample collection is in Karachi for now. We will add more cities.

What if I can't make my visit?

Reschedule or cancel free up to 24 hours before it. In the last 24 hours our lab calls you to arrange any change. If the visit has to move because of you then, or you are not home when we arrive, the first time is free; after that, visiting charges of 20% apply. Full details are in our refund and cancellation policy.

Is my health data private from my employer?

Always. If your company runs a health programme, HR sees only whether you booked and completed your check-up, never your results.
