Questions

Pricing questions

Is the app free?

Yes. Downloading and using PulsingLabs is free. You pay only for the check-up you book, and the price includes the home visit. There is no subscription.

Do I need to fast before the test?

No. Every test in both packages is non-fasting, so you can eat and drink as usual before your visit.

Who are the packages for?

Everyone, at any age. Standard is a clear first check-up; Premium adds 11 more tests for a fuller picture. You can also book for family members from your account.

Which cities do you cover?

Home sample collection is in Karachi for now. We will add more cities.

What if I can't make my visit?

Reschedule or cancel free up to 24 hours before it. In the last 24 hours our lab calls you to arrange any change. If the visit has to move because of you then, or you are not home when we arrive, the first time is free; after that, visiting charges of 20% apply. Full details are in our refund and cancellation policy.

Is my health data private from my employer?

Always. If your company runs a health programme, HR sees only whether you booked and completed your check-up, never your results.
