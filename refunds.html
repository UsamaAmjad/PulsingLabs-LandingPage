Refund & Cancellation

When you get your money back, clearly and up front.

Updated 23 October 2026Plain Englishcontact@pulsinglabs.com
On this page

    Last updated: 23 October 2026

    Life happens, and plans change. Here is exactly what happens when they do, in plain words. You see the same rules on the payment screen before you pay.

    Plans changed? Reschedule or cancel free up to 24 hours before your visit.

    Less than 24 hours to go? Our lab calls you to arrange any change.

    Can't make it, or not home when we arrive? Free the first time; after that, visiting charges of 20% apply.

    If we cancel, you get a full refund.

    1. Changing or cancelling your booking

    • More than 24 hours to go? Reschedule or cancel in the app, free. If you cancel, you get all your money back.
    • Less than 24 hours to go? The app can't change the visit any more, but don't worry: our lab will call you to sort it out.
    • Sample already collected? Then the booking can't be cancelled, because your tests have started.

    2. Visiting charges

    Sometimes a visit doesn't happen: you're not home when the phlebotomist arrives, or the visit has to move in the last 24 hours because of you (you ask to move it, or the lab can't reach you).

    • The first time: no charge. Just pick a new time.
    • After that: visiting charges of 20% of what you paid apply. Pay them in the app and pick a new time (what you already paid covers the rest), or cancel and get the other 80% back.

    Example: on a Standard booking of PKR 9,999, the visiting charges are PKR 2,000. If you cancel instead, you get PKR 7,999 back.

    3. When you get a full refund

    You get every rupee back when:

    • We or the lab cancel your visit.
    • Your payment went through but the booking could not be made.
    • You were charged twice for the same booking.

    And if your sample can't be tested because of a problem on our side or the lab's (for example, it is damaged or rejected), we come back and collect a new one, free.

    4. How refunds are paid

    Your money goes back to the card or wallet you paid with. We approve the refund and email you the amount; our payment provider then sends it, and how quickly it shows depends on your bank or wallet.

    5. Returns

    PulsingLabs sells a service, not goods, so there is nothing to return or ship back. Once your sample has been collected the service has been delivered and the booking can't be cancelled. If something went wrong with your visit, see sections 1 to 3 above, or write to us.

    6. Questions or a problem with a refund?

    Email contact@pulsinglabs.com with your booking number and we'll sort it out.
