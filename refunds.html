Refund & Cancellation

When you get your money back, clearly and up front.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

You pay for your test package in the app when you book. This page explains when you get your money back. The same rules are shown on the payment screen before you pay.

1. Cancelling your booking

  • 24 hours or more before your visit: full refund.
  • Less than 24 hours before your visit: we keep 30% of what you paid and refund the other 70%. By then a phlebotomist has been scheduled for you.
  • After your sample has been collected: the booking can't be cancelled, because testing has started.

Cancel from the Bookings tab in the app. If your visit is less than 24 hours away, the app tells you before you confirm.

Example: on a Standard booking of PKR 9,999, cancelling less than 24 hours before the visit keeps PKR 3,000 and refunds PKR 6,999.

2. If you are not home when the phlebotomist arrives

  • The first time: you can reschedule for free.
  • Any later time: a fee of 30% of what you paid applies. You can either pay the 30% in the app and pick a new time (the 70% you already paid covers the rest), or cancel and get the 70% back.

3. When you get a full refund

  • We or the lab cancel your visit.
  • Your payment went through but the booking could not be made.
  • You were charged twice for the same booking.

If your sample can't be tested because of a problem on our side or the lab's (for example, the sample is damaged or rejected), we collect a new sample at no cost.

4. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or wallet you paid with. We send you an email when we process it, showing the amount. It can take up to 2 weeks for the money to show in your account, depending on your bank or wallet.

5. Questions or a problem with a refund

Email contact@pulsinglabs.com with your booking number and we will sort it out.
