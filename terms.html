2. What PulsingLabs is

PulsingLabs is a booking app for health screening tests. A licensed partner laboratory collects your sample at your home, does the testing and produces your results. PulsingLabs takes your booking and payment, sends the booking to the lab, and shows you the results with plain-language explanations. PulsingLabs is not a laboratory and not a healthcare provider.

3. Not medical advice

The explanations, health scores and AI assistant in PulsingLabs are for information only. They do not diagnose any condition and are not a substitute for a doctor. Always talk to a qualified doctor about your results and before making decisions about your health. In an emergency, call 1122 or go to the nearest emergency department.

4. Your account

  • You must be 18 or older to create an account.
  • Give accurate details, especially your date of birth and sex: your results are judged against ranges for your age and sex.
  • Keep your phone and Google account secure, and don't share your account.
  • If you add and book for family members, you confirm you are allowed to share their details and receive their results.

5. Booking and payment

  • Prices are in Pakistani rupees and include home sample collection. The price is shown before you pay.
  • You pay the full price in the app when you book. Your booking is made once your payment succeeds.
  • Each payment gets an invoice, sent by email and available in the app.

6. The home visit

  • Be at the address you gave at the booked time. The phlebotomist will ask for the visit code shown in your app.
  • Follow any preparation shown for your tests.
  • Missed visits and cancellations follow our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

7. Your results

Your results are produced and signed by the partner laboratory, which is responsible for their accuracy. PulsingLabs shows them with explanations based on your age and sex; these ranges can differ from the ones the lab prints. If a value in the app ever differs from the lab's signed report, the lab's report is correct.

8. The AI health assistant

The assistant answers questions about your own results and helps with bookings. It can make mistakes and is not a doctor. It only changes a booking after you confirm it.
