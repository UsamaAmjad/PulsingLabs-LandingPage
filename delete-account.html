Delete your PulsingLabs account

Ask us to erase your account and your health data. No reinstall needed.

Updated 26 October 2026Plain Englishcontact@pulsinglabs.com
On this page

    Last updated: 26 October 2026

    This page is for the PulsingLabs app and website. You can ask us to delete your account and the data in it at any time, whether or not the app is still installed on your phone.

    1. How to ask

    Option A, by email (works even if you can't open the app): send a deletion request to contact@pulsinglabs.com from the email address you signed in with, or write the same words from any address and tell us the email on your account. We may reply with a question to be sure the account is yours.

    Option B, in the app: open Profile, tap Delete my account & data, and confirm. That sends us the same request.

    A person on our team reviews every request, so nobody can delete your account by mistake or on your behalf.

    2. What we erase

    • Your profile: name, phone, date of birth, sex, and any pregnancy details.
    • Your test results, lab report files and AI explanations.
    • Your wellbeing questionnaire answers.
    • Your AI chat history and any AI replies you reported.
    • The family members you added.
    • Your notification tokens, consent history and saved addresses.
    • Your sign-in. You are signed out everywhere and the account is closed.

    3. What we keep, and why

    • Payment records and invoices. The law requires us to keep financial records for tax and accounting. They keep the booking number, package, date and amount, without your health data, and are no longer linked to your name.
    • Encrypted backups. Backups made before your request are encrypted and are deleted on their own within 30 days. We do not use them for anything else.

    The partner lab that tested your sample keeps its own laboratory records, as the law requires of any laboratory. That is held by the lab, not by PulsingLabs; ask the lab if you want to know more.

    4. How long it takes

    We confirm by email that we received your request, and complete it as soon as we can, usually within 30 days. We email you again when your data has been erased.

    5. Before you go

    If you want a copy of your data first, open Profile in the app and choose Download my data. Deleting is permanent: we cannot restore an erased account.

    Questions? Email contact@pulsinglabs.com. How we handle your data in general is in the Privacy policy.

    Ready to delete?

    Send us the request. A person will confirm it by email.
