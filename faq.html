Using PulsingLabs
How do I book a test?

Pick a package, add your address, choose a time, then pay in the app. A phlebotomist comes to you. The whole flow takes a couple of minutes. See each step of a home visit.

Do you offer home sample collection?

Yes, every booking is a home visit in Karachi: a phlebotomist from a licensed partner lab comes to your home at your chosen time, and your results appear in the app.

Which cities do you cover?

Karachi, for now. We will add more cities.

Do I need to fast before the test?

No. Every test in both packages is non-fasting, so you can eat and drink as usual before your visit.

Who are the packages for?

Everyone, at any age. You can book for yourself and for family members from one account. Compare Standard and Premium.

What if I can't make my visit?

Reschedule or cancel free up to 24 hours before it. In the last 24 hours our lab calls you to arrange any change. If the visit has to move because of you then, or you are not home when we arrive, the first time is free; after that, visiting charges of 20% apply. See the refund and cancellation policy.

Can I import results from another lab?

Not yet. The app shows results from tests booked through PulsingLabs, so every value is read against the same age- and sex-aware ranges.

Can I manage my family's health too?

Yes. Add profiles for parents, kids and your spouse, each with age- and sex-aware reference ranges, all from one account.

Is the app available in Urdu?

The app itself is in English, but the AI health assistant can chat with you in Urdu if you prefer.

Results & AI
How are my results explained?

Each marker is plotted on a clinical zone chart (Normal / Borderline / High) tuned to your age and sex, with a plain-English explanation. The AI assistant can answer follow-up questions in everyday language.

Are the reference ranges trustworthy?

They follow evidence-based guidelines (ADA, AASLD, WHO, NICE and others), differentiated by age, sex and pregnancy, and are kept honest by an automated clinical test suite.

Can the AI give me medical advice?

It explains your results and general next steps, but it never prescribes drugs or doses, and it escalates urgent symptoms to emergency guidance. It's a guide, not a replacement for your doctor.

Privacy & billing
Is my health data private?

Yes. Your results are private to your account, encrypted in transit, and never sold. For companies, employees' individual results are never visible to HR, only anonymised aggregate trends.

What does it cost?

The app is free. You pay per check-up, with home sample collection included: Standard is PKR 9,999 (17 tests) and Premium is PKR 19,999 (27 tests). For a team programme, talk to us. See pricing.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. You can export your full account and results anytime, and request deletion, your data belongs to you.
