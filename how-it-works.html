Service & delivery policy

What you get, and how it reaches you

PulsingLabs is a service, not a shop. We don't ship anything: we come to you, and your results come to your phone.

What we provide

01

Booking a blood test at home and understanding your results. A licensed partner laboratory collects your sample and does the testing; we take care of the booking and payment, and show your results in the app with plain-language explanations.

  • Standard: PKR 9,999, 12 tests covering the essentials.
  • Premium: PKR 16,999, 23 tests: everything in Standard plus more.

Where we serve

02

Karachi, for now. We will add more cities and will update this page when we do.

Booking and payment

03
  • You pay online in the app (card or wallet) before the visit.
  • The booking is confirmed only once the payment has gone through.
  • Prices are in Pakistani rupees, include the home visit, and are shown before you pay.

Delivery: we come to you

04

Nothing is posted or couriered. The service is delivered at your home: a phlebotomist from a licensed partner lab collects your sample at your booked time. Please be at the address you gave.

Your results

05

Results come straight to the app, and we let you know when they're ready. You can download them at any time. If your sample can't be tested because of a problem on our side or the lab's, we collect a new one, free.

Your invoice

06

After you pay, we email you a receipt with your invoice attached. You can also open it in the app from your booking.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

07
  • 24 hours or more before: reschedule or cancel in the app, free. Cancelling gives a full refund.
  • In the last 24 hours: the app can't change it; our lab calls you to arrange any change.
  • Missed visits: if the visit has to move because of you in those 24 hours, or you are not home when we arrive, the first time is free. After that, visiting charges of 20% of what you paid apply.

Full details: refund and cancellation policy.

Your privacy

08

Your results are private to your account and never sold. If your employer runs a health programme, HR only sees whether you booked and completed your check-up, never your results. Privacy policy.

Common questions

Quick answers

How much does a home blood test cost in Karachi?

PKR 9,999 for Standard (12 tests) or PKR 16,999 for Premium (23 tests). The price includes the home visit; there are no extra collection charges.

Do I need to fast before a home blood test?

No. Every test in both packages is non-fasting, so you can eat and drink as usual.

Can children and older parents be tested?

Yes. The packages are for all ages, and results are explained using ranges for each person's age and sex.

Can I book for my parents from my account?

Yes. Add them as family members, then book a home visit for them. Each relative gets their own Health ID and results.

Which areas do you cover?

Karachi, for now. We will add more cities.
